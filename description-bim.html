Sectioning Tool (Slicing)

To enable exploration of the building's interior, we implemented high-performance Clipping Planes. This tool allows users to cut through the model in real-time along both horizontal (Y) and vertical (X) axes. By using local clipping in the Three.js renderer, we can reveal floor plans, structural CLT-elements, and internal layouts without losing the overall spatial context of the model.

Daylight Simulations

The Daylight Simulation engine calculates the sun's position based on a mathematical model of planetary motion. Users can simulate an entire day cycle (from 06:00 to 18:00). The simulation affects the global lighting, shadow casting, and sky color, providing critical visual data on how natural light interacts with the building's facade and surrounding landscape at different hours.

Building Information & Geometry

The model is rendered as a high-fidelity GLTF/GLB asset, ensuring a smooth web experience while maintaining architectural detail. Instead of static images, the viewer provides a 360-degree interactive environment where technical data—such as energy performance (U-values) and structural systems—is presented in a dedicated side-panel, giving a clear overview of the building's "As-Built" specifications.

Basemap & Geolocation

Context is key in urban management. The BIM viewer utilizes an ArcGIS Satellite Basemap that is dynamically tiled around the building's coordinates. By combining high-resolution satellite imagery with GeoJSON-based road and building data from Jönköping Municipality, we bridge the gap between a standalone architectural model and its real-world urban environment.

Lessons for Future Projects

Throughout the project, we discovered that Gemini was not always optimal for specific coding tasks and technical troubleshooting. We also tested alternative tools like Lovable and Codex, which showed great potential. However, usage of these tools was limited because they only offer a certain number of free tokens per day, meaning we could not utilize their full capacity for this project.
